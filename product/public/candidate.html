    A moment for your privacy.

    During this interview, short windows of your voice are checked to make sure the same person keeps speaking:

    • You will record a short voice reference now. Later answers are compared against it. This checks voice continuity only — it is not proof of your real-world identity.
    • Audio is also checked for signs of synthetic (AI-generated) voice. Both checks are performed by DetectifAI.
    • Short audio windows are kept as evidence for the reviewer. If you allow the camera, small snapshots and short low-resolution video clips are also kept so the reviewer can see the moments around a flagged answer; video older than about two minutes is discarded unless it is near a flagged moment. Everything is deleted under the retention policy. Video is context for a person, never used for automated detection.
    • A human reviewer makes every decision. Nothing is rejected automatically.
